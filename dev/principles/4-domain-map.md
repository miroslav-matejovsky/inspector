# Domain Map

The Inspector Domain is organized around five core domains that together enable understanding of a system. Each domain addresses a distinct aspect of inspection while remaining independent of any particular technology, architecture or business problem.

## Observation

Observation is responsible for exposing the current state of a system.

It provides visibility into what exists, what is known, and how information is currently represented. Observation is concerned with facts rather than interpretation.

Core questions:

```text
What exists?
What is its current state?
What does the system currently know?
```

---

## Explanation

Explanation is responsible for making system behaviour understandable.

It helps users understand why objects, relationships, states and outcomes exist by exposing the reasons, dependencies and causes behind them.

Core questions:

```text
Why does this exist?
Why is it in this state?
Why did this happen?
```

---

## Navigation

Navigation is responsible for traversing the model and discovering context.

It allows users to move between related concepts, follow dependencies and explore how information is connected throughout the system.

Core questions:

```text
What is related?
How is this connected?
Where did this come from?
```

---

## Representation

Representation is responsible for presenting inspection information in forms that support understanding.

It transforms observations, explanations and relationships into views that can be consumed by humans or other tools.

Core questions:

```text
How should this be presented?
How can understanding be communicated?
```

---

## Connectivity

Connectivity is responsible for integrating Inspector with external systems.

It provides access to the sources from which inspection information is obtained while remaining independent of specific protocols, platforms and technologies.

Core questions:

```text
Where does the information come from?
How is it accessed?
How is it connected?
```

---

Together these domains support the three fundamental principles of Inspector:

```text
Observe
Explain
Navigate
```

The purpose of Inspector is to help users understand a system by observing its state, explaining its behaviour and navigating its relationships.
